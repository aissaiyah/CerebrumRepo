# AuscultateLungs.txt - Auscultate lung sounds with stethoscope
# Player uses stethoscope to listen to patient lung sounds

$PlayerNurse play LeanIn
Wait 1

Prompt 'Placing stethoscope on patient chest...'
Wait 2

# Audio cue - wheezing sounds
Prompt 'Listening to anterior lung fields...'
Wait 2

Prompt 'Listening to posterior lung fields...'
Wait 2

Prompt 'Auscultation complete.'
Wait 1

$PlayerNurse release
Prompt ''
Return
